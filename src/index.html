FAQ

Brief answers ahead of commitment.

Must a token launch precede the board?

No. The board consumes its feed independently, whatever launch status applies.

Where will our contract address appear?

Inside the bar pinned beneath the header. Reads pending until confirmation, afterward becomes a tap-to-copy field.

Which chain does Cursonic target?

Solana. The rails strip above names the chain plus the endpoint queried.

Why are some links gray?

A gray icon marks a channel not yet announced. A link appears solely after the channel exists.

Does anything leave my machine?

Only following a deliberate push. Chat history, keys, logs sit inside a local directory.

Will approvals ever be skipped?

Hazardous steps wait inside a queue. Nothing exits before release.

Should the board count as advice?

No. Raw public figures, displayed unaltered.
